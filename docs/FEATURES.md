# Feature guide

Use the names in the header to check who will be affected. The selected character receives character edits; Other is used by two-person tools. Major actions open a review.

## Move the Cheats button

Drag the grip to the left of **Cheats** to move the button. Click **Cheats** to open the window. The button hides while **Decisions** is open and returns when you close it.

## Body and identity

Open **Character → Body & identity**. Increase or decrease the selected adult’s current weight by 25, or reset it to the game’s midpoint. Weight can still change over time. The same page can set sexuality to heterosexual, homosexual, bisexual or asexual.

## Debug mode

Open **Tools & editors → Enable CK3 debug mode** to turn on CK3’s debug console and tooltips for the current session. Click **Disable CK3 debug mode** in the same place to turn it off again. Oddbyte works with either setting.

## Ruler standing

- Tools & editors > Legitimacy, dread and tyranny, also linked from Character.
- Add/remove 100 or 500 legitimacy points for a playable ruler. Native limits apply;
  these are points, not a chosen number of legitimacy levels.
- Add/remove 10 dread, or use the number pad to set dread to 0–100. Requires a ruler.
  Traits and active boosts can raise dread again.
- Clear tyranny removes the selected ruler's current tyranny. Other opinion penalties,
  rivalries and grievances remain.

## Languages and cultural acceptance

- Selected learns the language of Other's actual culture, including campaign-created cultures.
  Requires age 6+ and an unknown language; no learning scheme or culture conversion is created.
- Add 10/25 acceptance points, or set acceptance to 100, between the two characters' cultures.
  This affects the shared relationship between both whole cultures. Identical cultures are excluded.
- Reviews name the original cultures. Apply is disabled if either character changes culture.

## Dynasty legacies

- Open from Tools & editors or Family and dynasty. Unlock an individual perk without spending renown.
- Earlier perks in the same track must be unlocked first. Normal DLC and dynasty-head eligibility apply.
- The whole selected dynasty benefits. A review is invalidated if the selected character changes dynasty.
- Perks requiring a trait choice offer one button per choice, including Architected Ancestry.
  The choice is stored for the native legacy effects; it does not immediately grant that trait to everyone.
- Already unlocked perks are marked and cannot be granted again. No removal or undo is provided.

## Education and guardians

- Selected is the child; Other is the guardian. Assign Other to a child under 16 with no guardian.
  The guardian must be free, capable, aged 18+ and have fewer than two wards.
- Guardian assignment/removal requires both people at home at the same court, with no pending
  education offer or travel. Use the normal game interaction for education in another court.
- To replace a guardian, select that guardian as Other and remove them, then select the replacement.
  Other guardianships stay. No forced faith/culture conversion is added.
- Choose diplomacy, martial, stewardship, intrigue or learning education for a child aged 6–15.
  Native focus-change effects run and can adjust existing education progress. This does not grant
  a completed education trait. Use the trait browser to change a finished education.

## Alliances

- Open from Tools & editors or Diplomacy. Selected and Other are the two named rulers.
- Create an alliance between different free, landed rulers who are not enemies in a war
  or in each other's liege chain. Negotiation and marriage requirements are bypassed.
- The alliance is tied to these rulers and follows native death/succession rules.
  No war is joined automatically. A deliberately broken alliance can be created again.
- Break alliance affects only that pair. Native consequences apply; current wars are not ended.

## Search, favourites and targets

- Search: click letters to find the start of a word, for example GEN, COURT or GOLD.
  Clear shows all choices. This is an on-screen letter selector, not a typed text field.
- Click a star beside a listed action to save it in Favourites. Starred actions use the
  current named character(s), amount and picker selection. They keep all normal guards
  and confirmations. Classic search results include their page name to distinguish similar actions.
- Pinned characters: eight named slots. Save selected stores/replaces a slot; Select edits
  that person; Other chooses them for two-character tools; Clear empties the slot.
  Dead pins cannot be selected. Pins and favourites are saved on your current player ruler;
  choose them again after succession or switching player characters if needed.
- Active boosts shows removable construction, health/fertility and preset boosts on the
  selected character. County boost removal remains in Realm & buildings. This is not an
  undo history for resource, base-skill, age, trait-opposite, family or ownership changes.
- Hover over an unavailable action for its requirements. Major changes review the original
  subject, other person, picked object and number. Long reviews scroll. Cancel changes nothing.

## Full trait browser

- Character > Browse all traits: all 311 native traits, with categories, search,
  an Active filter and current-state labels. Add/remove traits directly. Adding respects native age limits and removes
  incompatible traits. Education changes preserve
  separate prowess education.
- Traits with experience tracks have +100 XP buttons for each native track. Requires the
  trait to be present. Normal track caps apply. Third-party mods' added traits are not indexed.

## Exact values

- Resources/Character > Set exact values, or Tools & editors > Set exact values.
- Clear, then click number-pad digits, use quick values, or delete the last digit.
  Enter 0–999,999 for gold, prestige, piety or dynasty renown. Renown affects the dynasty.
  Existing fame/devotion/legacy progression is not reset by setting a balance.
- Set any of six BASE skills to 0–100. Trait and modifier bonuses stay, so totals can be
  higher. Exact skill edits and the window's +/- skill rows refresh totals immediately.
- Set age to 16–120. This bypasses birthday effects; family links remain and age-related
  health may not recalculate immediately. Set capital settlement size to 0–10.
  Renown/settlement-size reviews name the original dynasty/settlement and disable Apply if the
  character changes dynasty or moves their capital. Exact values have no undo button.

## Court and prisoners

- Selected character is the ruler/jailer; Other is the person affected.
- Bring a free, unlanded non-ruler into the selected ruler's court. Imprison a free other
  character, or release someone imprisoned by the selected ruler without a ransom.
- Appoint an adult courtier as chancellor, marshal, steward or spymaster, replacing the
  existing holder. Dismiss a councillor first before appointing them to another council job.
- Appoint/replace a court physician, or remove the other character from that position.
- Force, forbid or restore normal knight preference for an adult courtier. These preferences
  do not bypass normal knight eligibility. Check the result in the normal Knights window.
  Native consequences and events still run for prison and appointment actions.

## Family and dynasty

- Marry two unrelated unmarried adults aged 18+, including a matrilineal option; divorce
  existing spouses. These controls currently create opposite-sex marriages.
- Move the selected character into the other person's house/dynasty. Descendants are not
  moved automatically. House membership and marriage can change succession.
- Adopt an unrelated unlanded child under 16; the selected adult must be at least 16 years
  older. The child joins the parent's house when one exists. Parentage/inheritance can change.
- Designate a living direct child as heir. Title-specific succession laws can still affect
  what they inherit.
- Break the engagement between Selected and Other, or found a cadet branch for a landed adult feudal house member. Eligible descendants join the new house.

## Titles, vassals and contracts

- Right-click a title owner > Cheat Menu: Edit a Title. Pick a personally held county-or-
  higher title. Back in Titles and vassals, take the title for the selected character,
  either alone or with its vassals. Ownership changes only after Apply. For a duchy or higher,
  give an unlanded recipient a county first.
- Claims remain a separate system. Title transfer changes ownership and can change rank,
  government, succession and realm borders. There is no undo button.
- Make a lower-rank landed Other ruler your selected ruler's vassal, or make a selected
  landed vassal independent. Ancestors in the liege chain are excluded from vassalization.
- Set feudal tax/levy obligations from none to maximum. Requires a modifiable feudal vassal
  contract. This edits the selected vassal's contract with their own liege; Other is not used.
- Open **Tools & editors → Titles, vassals and contracts → Realm succession** to set Confederate Partition, Partition, High Partition, Primogeniture or Ultimogeniture. Requires an eligible feudal or clan ruler and the law’s native unlocks. Individual title laws can still affect inheritance.

## Wars and truces

- Military > Wars and truces, or open it from Tools & editors. Pick one of the selected
  character's active wars. Refresh if the list has changed.
- Win or Lose refers to that character's SIDE, including when participating as an ally.
  White peace is available only for a war that supports it. Only the chosen war is ended.
- Normal war settlement, rewards, title changes and new truces apply.
- Clear truces removes truces in both directions between the named pair only.

## Culture, faith and rites

- Choose from 255 cultures or 104 faith types to change only the selected person.
  Match Other copies the person's actual culture, faith and rite, including campaign-created
  cultures and rites. Counties and relatives are not forcibly converted.
- Discover one or all 133 innovations for the selected person's culture.
  Add/remove 207 cultural traditions. Adding needs a free tradition slot. These changes
  affect everyone of that culture; normal technology/adoption costs and time are bypassed.
- CK3 uses rites for active religious rules. The doctrine/tenet editors name the selected
  character's actual rite and affect its followers; other rites keep their own rules.
- Choose among 126 doctrine choices; setting one replaces alternatives in its own group.
  Add/remove 97 tenets. A core tenet needs a free slot (normally three total); remove one
  of the displayed current core tenets before choosing its replacement. Locked tenets require
  their DLC; unavailable Add buttons explain the requirement.

## Artifacts

- Choose an owned artifact from the list, then repair it, improve it or give it to Other.
- Improve sets illustrious rarity and one cheat craftsmanship bonus: +10 prowess,
  +3 diplomacy and +2 monthly prestige while equipped. Existing bonuses stay; repeating
  does not stack the cheat bonus. The original model and internal quality score remain unchanged.
- Create a sword, armor, crown, regalia or ring in common/masterwork/famed/illustrious rarity.
  Creation sets a matching quality score and craftsmanship bonus. Items appear in inventory;
  they are not automatically equipped. Normal equipment and DLC eligibility still apply.
  Crown creation uses a base-game model if the normal cosmetic variant is unavailable.

## Create a character

- Select a landed ruler, then open Create a character. Set the chosen number with the
  number pad. Use it as age (18–80) or copy it to any of the six base skills (0–100).
- Choose male/female and one role: courtier, commander, physician, councillor or knight.
  Roles supply a suitable education; education and random traits can add to the base skills.
- The person joins the selected ruler's court with their culture, faith and rite. They are not
  automatically married or appointed. Select the last created character opens their editor.


## Diplomacy

- **Learn selected character’s secrets** privately reveals unknown secrets to your ruler. Find the results in CK3’s **Intrigue** window.

- Hooks: give the selected character a ten-year weak hook or twenty-year strong hook
  on Other, or remove their hook on Other. A weak grant preserves existing hooks;
  a strong grant upgrades a weaker one and preserves an existing strong hook.
  Hook removal can remove a native hook as well as one created by this mod.
- Opinion: apply +100 / +50 / -50 / -100 to Other's opinion of the selected character
  for ten years. These are modifiers, not a forced final opinion value. Our adjustment
  replaces itself instead of stacking. Remove clears only this mod's opinion adjustment.
- Relationships: add/remove Friend, Best friend, Rival, Nemesis, Lover and Soulmate.
  Friend/rival additions remove their opposite for the pair. Romantic additions require
  unrelated adults aged 18+. Native relationship effects and notifications still run.

## Claims

- Right-click a landed ruler > Cheat Menu: Claim a Title. Pick one of their personally
  held county-or-higher titles, confirm, then choose a pressed or unpressed claim.
  Your own ruler receives the claim by default. No claim changes until that final choice.
- Right-click a ruler > Cheat Menu: Claims on This Ruler for primary-title or all-personal-
  title grants, with both pressed and unpressed options.
- For another claimant: edit that character, open Claims & titles > Who receives portrait claims?,
  and choose Use this character for portrait claims. Then choose the title owner by portrait.
  Portrait claim menus use the pinned living character; their name and portrait are
  shown as the beneficiary. Reset the claimant to return portrait tools to your own ruler.
  The pin stays with your current player ruler; a dead/missing pin falls back to that ruler.
- In Claims and titles, bulk shortcuts act on the currently selected character: claim
  their immediate liege's primary/all personal titles, or neighboring top-level rulers'
  primary/all personal titles. Neighbor actions do not claim every title in those realms.
- Upgrade all explicit unpressed claims to pressed. Remove one selected explicit claim,
  or review and confirm removal of all explicit claims. Implicit family claims stay.
- Repeated grants do not stack. Unpressed grants preserve existing pressed claims.
  Owned titles, baronies, and landless titles are excluded from grants.
Claims do not transfer ownership or start wars. The game's normal claim-use, inheritance,
war, government and religion rules still apply. Claim changes persist when saved.


## Boosts and buildings

- Four removable preset traits: Character boost, Realm boost, Commander boost,
  and a combined preset. Each adds 100 to all six skills. The combined preset includes
  the three packages with one icon; its skill bonus is included once. Individual presets
  can coexist, and the combined preset replaces them. Immortality remains separate.
- Character boost: very large health/fertility, monthly income/piety/prestige/renown,
  stress reduction, dread, capacity, attraction, culture-opinion and modern scheme bonuses.
- Realm boost: extremely fast/cheap construction, larger levies, higher holding taxes,
  rapid settlement growth/control and +100 popular opinion.
- Commander boost: battle rolls, advantage, casualty protection, travel, sieges, raiding,
  supply and siege-regiment capacity bonuses.
- Apply presets to existing living close family and spouses. Family immortality includes
  adult visual age 25 and a removal button. Future family members are not auto-buffed.
- Individual resource menus include +100,000. The resource bundle grants dynasty
  renown in 1,000 / 10,000 / 100,000 increments and a 100,000-of-all-four bundle.
- Convert personal counties or the entire selected sub-realm to that character's faith,
  culture, or both. The sub-realm option also converts vassals at every level.
- Expanded healing removes illness-tagged disease traits and negative congenital
  conditions. Separate buttons and a family option are supplied. Giant is preserved.
- Bulk building tools target personal holdings or all sub-realm holdings. Expand to seven
  regular district slots, apply available building upgrades, fill eligible regular district
  and building slots, or combine all three. Review the target scope before applying.
  These tools are free and instant. Existing buildings stay; larger slot capacities stay larger.
  Florin's holding, placement and unlock rules still apply, so some slots may remain empty.
  Existing special and duchy buildings can be upgraded. Great projects, trade posts,
  entrepôts and domiciles are excluded from bulk building.

## Character, county and military tools

- Selected-character editing: character cheats apply to the named subject while
  you operate the menu. Includes existing resources, stress, skills, traits and construction.
- Lifestyle experience: add 250 / 1,000 / 5,000 experience in diplomacy, martial,
  stewardship, intrigue, learning or wanderer.
- Lifestyle perk points: add 1 / 5 / 10 unspent points in any of those six lifestyles.
  Choose the perks in the normal lifestyle screen. Normal character/DLC eligibility applies.
- Age: make a character 1 / 5 / 10 years younger or older. Younger buttons are only
  offered when the result is at least 16. Changing age does not reverse earlier life events.
- Health: toggle a removable +5 health boost or remove common ailments.
- Fertility: choose a removable +100% or -100% modifier, or remove both cheats.
  Normal fertility, pregnancy, age and relationship rules still apply.
- County controls: select the capital county or all county titles personally held by the
  selected character; vassal counties are excluded. Add 1 / 5 / 10 settlement size to the
  county seat, restore control, or enable/remove +100 popular opinion. Size and control use normal caps.
  The opinion modifier does not stack with itself and stays on the county until removed.
- Military: fill existing men-at-arms to their current troop capacity; replenish personal
  holding levy pools and garrisons. No regiments or additional capacity are created.
- **Military → Create men-at-arms** adds one size-1 regiment of Light Footmen, Bowmen or Onagers for free. The selected ruler needs a free regiment slot and access to that unit. Normal upkeep applies.
- Commanders: add 5 martial, 5 prowess or both. Add/remove Aggressive Attacker,
  Unyielding Defender, Logistician, Military Engineer, Organizer and Flexible Leader.
  Select a different commander through their portrait to edit them.
Unlanded characters have no personal counties or holding levies to change. Characters
without men-at-arms have no regiments to refill. Ineligible actions are hidden where practical.

## Resources, skills and construction

- Gold, prestige and piety: add 100 / 1,000 / 10,000 / 100,000 per click.
- Stress: remove up to 1,000 stress, taking a normal stress bar to zero.
- Skills: add 1 / 5 to all six skills, or 1 / 5 / 10 to an individual skill.
- Congenital traits: Genius, Herculean/Amazonian, Beautiful, Fecund, Pure-blooded.
- Personality traits: Brave, Diligent, Temperate, Patient, Gregarious, Ambitious.
- Other traits: Strong, Shrewd, Athletic, Immortal.
- Common ailment removal: illness, three wound levels, infirmity, incapability,
  depression, lunacy and possession, including genetic variants where applicable.
  Other diseases and physical injuries are unchanged.
- Near-instant construction: toggle for the selected character's own buildings, districts,
  new holdings and domicile upgrades. Costs, prerequisites and slots remain normal.
  Exact time follows the game's minimum and project. Start a new project after enabling
  the modifier if an existing queue retains its old timer.

## Saves and modifiers

The menu is intended for single-player. Character changes persist when saved, including
resources, skills, age, perk points and traits. Adding a trait removes incompatible traits;
removing it does not restore previously replaced traits. Settlement size and county control edits persist.
Health, fertility, popular-opinion and construction cheats have removal buttons. Remove
unwanted modifiers before disabling this mod. Character modifiers stay with the character;
select a successor and enable them again if desired.
