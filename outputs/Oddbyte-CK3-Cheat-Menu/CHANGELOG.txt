ODDBYTE — CK3 CHEAT MENU 1.7.0

Adds a drag grip to the Cheats button. Adds men-at-arms recruitment, weight,
sexuality, betrothal, cadet branch, succession law and private secret tools.

1.6.3

Adds a CK3 debug-mode toggle under Tools & editors.

1.6.2

Hides the floating Cheats button while Decisions is open so it cannot block
clicks on decision rows. Compatible with the latest CK3 1.21 Florin hotfix.
Playtested on macOS and Windows.

1.6.1

Updated for CK3 1.21 Florin. Refreshes catalogs, replaces development with settlement
size and updates realm boosts and construction for districts. Bulk construction is
free and instant within Florin's normal placement and unlock rules. Reviews saved
in an older mod version must be cancelled and opened again before applying a cheat.

1.6.0

Adds ruler standing, languages/cultural acceptance, dynasty legacies, education/
guardians and alliance tools. New actions use Search, Favourites and original-target
reviews. Legacy unlocks preserve renown and store required trait choices. Native
eligibility and track order still apply. Guardian editing is limited to available
people at the same court. Local installation preserves the Steam Workshop ID.

1.5.2

Oddbyte branding, refreshed public guides and configurable development paths.
Gameplay and saved identifiers are retained from 1.5.1.

The gameplay baseline includes fixes for expired references, faith/rite copying, creator
rite inheritance, trait ages, tenet DLC requirements, artifact visuals, higher-title
recipients, original-context reviews and named trait XP actions.
