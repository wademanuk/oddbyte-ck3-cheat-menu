ODDBYTE — CK3 CHEAT MENU: TESTING

Version 1.6.2 works with the latest CK3 1.21 Florin hotfix.
Playtested on macOS and Windows. 108 regression tests and 6,248 native checks passed.
No mod-specific script errors or GUI warnings in the final native run.
Coverage includes targets, reviews, catalogs, titles, contracts, artifacts, war outcomes,
ruler standing, languages/acceptance, legacy choices and renown/level preservation,
education/guardians, alliances, Florin settlement size and district construction, stale
confirmation rejection and local Workshop ID preservation during upgrades.
Eligible DLC actions are exercised; unavailable actions are checked as disabled.
This is not exhaustive campaign or other-mod validation. Knight preference display, UI
scales, save/reload, long campaigns and multiplayer remain outside the full automated coverage.
The engine harness does not click through the rendered window.

Before a playtest, make a separate save. Check selections, Cancel/Apply, long panels,
war/artifact/title pickers and save/reload with your normal playset.

Report bugs: https://github.com/wademanuk/oddbyte-ck3-cheat-menu/issues
See the repository docs/TESTING.md for the methodology and reporting checklist.
